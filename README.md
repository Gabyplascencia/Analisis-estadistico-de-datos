# Revenue from telephone plan, Megaline
Megaline provides us with data to determine which of the two phone plans generates the most profit, and whether customers have the package that best suits them or which ones could be changed for the benefit of both parties.

## 🏁Objetive
Testing the Hypotheses:

- The average income of Ultimate and Surf plan users differs.
- The average income of users in the New York-New Jersey area is different from that of users in other regions.

## 🖥Technologies used
- **Python**: Pandas, mat, plotlib, numpy, seaborn, scipy, matplotlib.
- **Jupyter Notebook**: Interactive environment for analysis.
- **CSV Dataset**: Contains user behavior data.

## 🔑Key Steps
1. **Data Description**:
   - Assessed the quality of the dataset and summarized its structure.
2. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Handled missing values, corrected formatting issues, and removed duplicates.
3. **Hypothesis Testing**:
   - Compared user activity between cities and across different days of the week using statistical methods.


## 🥇🏆Results
The analysis confirms that:
- Surf Plan is the one that gives the most income to the company.
- The hypothesis test says that despite the differences, there is no difference in income for the number of clients that each plan has.
- The hypothesis test says that despite the differences, there is no difference in income between the two cities analyzed.
